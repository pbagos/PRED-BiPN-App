METHOD INFORMATION

Disparity Filter aims to extract the multiscale backbone of a weighted bipartite network by retaining edges whose weights cannot be explained by random fluctuations. The method applies a local statistical comparison at each node, evaluating edge weights against a null model based on a Beta distribution. Edges with p-values lower than the significance level α are preserved, ensuring that important connections — especially those involving weak nodes — are retained.

This method needs a file (.csv or .tsv) with three columns (Source  Target  Weight) representing a weighted one-mode projected undirected network. If your data is bipartite (2 columns), use the Simple Weighted Projection method first.

Reference: Serrano, M. Á., Boguñá, M., & Vespignani, A. (2009).
PNAS, 106(16), 6483–6488
